Support

Questions, bug reports or feedback about any of our apps? Email arnoreli@outlook.com. We usually reply within 2 business days.

What to include

  • The app name and version (Settings → General → iPhone Storage → the app)
  • Your device model and iOS version
  • What you expected, what happened, and a screenshot if you can
  • For a content error: the lesson or question text, so we can find it
